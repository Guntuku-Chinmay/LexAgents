RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This lease agreement is entered into on January 1, 2025, between LANDLORD: Charles Smith, and TENANT: Jane Doe, for the property located at 456 Oak Street, Los Angeles, CA 90025.

TERMS:
1. Rent: Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $3,000, due on the 1st of each calendar month.
2. Security Deposit: Tenant shall deposit the sum of $2,500 with the Landlord as security for any damage caused to the premises during the term of this lease.
3. Return of Deposit: Upon termination of this lease, the Landlord shall inspect the premises and return the security deposit. Under Section 10 of this agreement, the Landlord may deduct unpaid rent, cleaning costs, and costs for repairs exceeding normal wear and tear.
4. Timeline for Refund: The Landlord agrees to return the remaining security deposit and/or provide an itemized list of deductions to the Tenant within 30 days of the Tenant vacating the premises.
5. Dispute Resolution: Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be submitted to mediation prior to filing any legal action.

Signed,
Landlord: Charles Smith
Tenant: Jane Doe
