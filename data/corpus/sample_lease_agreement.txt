INDIAN COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on January 1, 2026, at New Delhi, India, between LANDLORD: Rajesh Kumar, and TENANT: Amit Verma, for the commercial premises located at 12 Barakhamba Road, Connaught Place, New Delhi 110001.

TERMS & COVENANTS:

Section 1. Payment Mode:
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 1,50,000, due on the 1st of each calendar month. The payment shall be made exclusively via post-dated cheques drawn on the Tenant's bank account. Under Section 10 of this agreement, the Landlord shall present the cheque on the due date. If any cheque is returned unpaid due to insufficient funds, the Tenant shall immediately pay the amount, and any delay beyond 30 days of notice shall entitle the Landlord to initiate immediate eviction proceedings.

Section 2. Surveillance & Privacy Waiver:
The Landlord retains the right to install digital CCTV cameras and network sniffers inside the private workspace of the premises to monitor all communications and data packets of the Tenant's staff. The Tenant hereby waives all rights to personal digital privacy, including any protections claimed under Article 21 of the Constitution of India or Puttaswamy judgment guidelines, and consents to constant monitoring of messages.

Section 3. Dispute Resolution:
All disputes arising out of this agreement shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the Indian Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The place of arbitration shall be New Delhi, and proceedings shall be in English.
